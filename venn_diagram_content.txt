WHY USE BOTH DTS AND DAS?
Complementary Distributed Fiber Optic Sensing

LEFT CIRCLE (DTS - Blue):
• Physical Parameter: Temperature
• Measurement: Thermal profiling
• Applications:
  - Leak detection (thermal signatures)
  - Geothermal monitoring
  - Groundwater flow
  - Thermal stress analysis

RIGHT CIRCLE (DAS - Green):
• Physical Parameter: Strain/Vibration
• Measurement: Acoustic/seismic
• Applications:
  - Structural health monitoring
  - Seismic activity detection
  - Mechanical failure prediction
  - Ground movement

OVERLAP SECTION (Darker Blue-Green):
• Single 9/125 µm fiber
• Simultaneous measurement
• Enhanced leak detection (thermal + acoustic)
• Comprehensive monitoring
• Cost-effective dual sensing
• Early warning systems

KEY ADVANTAGE: "Two measurements, one fiber - complete subsurface intelligence"

BOTTOM APPLICATIONS:
• Geothermal: Thermal gradients + seismic activity
• Infrastructure: Thermal stress + structural vibration
• Environmental: Groundwater flow + subsurface movement
